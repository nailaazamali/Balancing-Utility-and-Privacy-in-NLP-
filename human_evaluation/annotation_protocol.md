# Human evaluation protocol

Two annotators independently reviewed each selected counterfactual.

For each case, they recorded:

1. whether the counterfactual contains sensitive information;
2. whether any sensitive information introduced in the counterfactual is supported by the source text; and
3. whether the counterfactual keeps adequate explanatory strength.

Initial agreement was calculated before the annotators discussed disagreements. The paper reports 450 reviewed outputs, 412 initial agreements (91.6%), Cohen's κ = 0.831, and 38 disagreements resolved after discussion.

The row-level annotation records are not included here. `annotation_template.csv` is a blank version of the final annotation format.
